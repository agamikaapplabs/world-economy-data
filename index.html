How to read the dashboard

Observed vs inferred

Observed values come from the cited source. Correlations, rotation proxies, diagnostics and Market Pulse are calculated locally and are labelled as interpretation rather than source facts.

Different update speeds

Market prices can update daily, high-frequency India indicators monthly, and World Bank macro data annually. The observation period matters more than the time the file was downloaded.

Missing means missing

If a reliable comparable series is unavailable, the dashboard leaves it blank or marks it pending instead of replacing it with a price proxy or an invented estimate.

Collection & Calculation Notes

Market daily change

Where history is available, the application recomputes the daily move from the last two actual observations rather than relying blindly on a provider's chartPreviousClose field.

Normalized charts

Normalization rebases each series to 100 at the start of the selected period. It does not create a synthetic index and it does not convert currencies.

Connections

Connections use Pearson correlation of overlapping daily returns. A high correlation is evidence of co-movement, not proof that one asset caused another to move.

Capital rotation proxy

The proxy compares recent group price performance. It does not establish that the same investors moved money from one group to another; direct flow data is a future layer.
